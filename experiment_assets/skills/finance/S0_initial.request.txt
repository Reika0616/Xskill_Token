I want you to give me the final amount in USD obtained after these transactions.

Golden Answer:
To identify the head-and-shoulders pattern, we look for three consecutive peaks: a left shoulder, a higher central peak (the head), and a right shoulder roughly equal in height to the first. In this case, the pattern appears between September and December 2021. At the beginning of September, we see the first shoulder with the nearest multiple of 4,000 at 52,000 USD. The head reaches about 68,000 USD in mid-November, and finally the second shoulder forms at the end of December, again around 52,000 USD.

For the calculations, we start with 1.5 BTC and add 20,000 USD at a price of 52,000 USD, following the nearest-multiple rule.
- 20,000 ÷ 52,000 ≈ 0.3846 BTC
Total BTC after purchase = 1.5 + 0.3846 ≈ 1.8846 BTC
At the head (68,000 USD), we sell 60% of the holdings:
- 1.8846 × 0.6 = 1.1308 BTC
-1.1308 × 68,000 ≈ 76,923 USD
- Remaining BTC = 1.8846 − 1.1308 = 0.7538 BTC
At the second shoulder (52,000 USD), we sell the rest:
-0.7538 × 52,000 ≈ 39,538 USD

Final total = 76,923 + 39,538 ≈ 116,461 USD
